How scoring works

Every finish scores out of 100. 1st = 100, last = 0, and everything between is scaled to the size of the match. So 4th of 12 scores 73, and 2nd of 2 scores 0.

Your rating is your average score, so it doesn't matter if you've played 10 matches or 50. Better, not more, wins.

Ties share the place (a 1v1 draw = 50 each). You need 3 matches to qualify for a crown, so one lucky race can't take it.
